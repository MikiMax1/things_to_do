# The original Ashveil

These four files are the original version of the game, kept exactly as they
were so you can still open `legacy/index.html` and play it, or lift ideas
back out of it.

The new game lives one directory up. It is a full rewrite — canvas renderer,
a real economy with workers, a tech tree, and battles you watch — but the
bones came from here: seasons, a rival kingdom called Brannoch, a quest
board, and buildings on a grid.
